Examples:

circles
rectangles

Voronoi

Recommended development stack

Given your background, I'd use:

Language: C++
Graphics: raylib
Audio: miniaudio
Image loading (if needed): stb_image
Math: raymath (included with raylib)
Build system: CMake
Compiler: Clang (often produces slightly smaller binaries than GCC with aggressive optimisation, though results vary)
Optimisation: -Os or -Oz (where supported), link-time optimisation (LTO), strip symbols, and, if permitted, UPX packing.
If your goal is to win

Many successful entries share these traits:

Procedurally generated graphics instead of image assets.
Procedural music and sound effects.
Minimal external libraries.
One self-contained executable.
Simple but polished gameplay with a distinctive mechanic.
Strong visual style despite the tiny size.

A polished game that's only 500 KB often scores better than a technically impressive 1.43 MB project because judges typically value originality and fun over sheer technical complexity.

Given your interests in AI, procedural generation, and physics-based projects, a procedurally generated roguelike, survival game, or physics puzzle built with C++ + raylib would play directly to your strengths while fitting comfortably within the contest constraints.
